About me

Every number tells a story. I want mine to be one of impact.

I started my career in accounting, where I learned to read the story behind the numbers and to care about getting them right. Now I'm pursuing a Master of Science in Computational Finance and Risk Management at the University of Washington, bringing that foundation into analyst, risk management and quantitative roles.

Growing up in Indonesia and building a life and career abroad taught me to adapt quickly, work hard and stay resilient when things get difficult. Leading a clean water project back home showed me how much careful planning and honest numbers can change people's lives.

That's what draws me to the energy sector and to non-profit work: industries where careful analysis can shape a better future. I want to know that at the end of the day, I've helped build something meaningful. I'm also open to other opportunities where I can grow and contribute.
