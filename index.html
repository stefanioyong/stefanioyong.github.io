About me

I started my career in accounting, where I learned to read the story behind the numbers, and to care about getting them right. Now I'm pursuing a Master of Science in Computational Finance and Risk Management at the University of Washington, bringing that foundation into analyst, risk management and quantitative roles.

Growing up in Indonesia and building a life and career abroad taught me to adapt quickly, work hard and stay resilient when things get difficult. I want my work to matter: to know that at the end of the day, I've helped build something meaningful.

That's what draws me to the energy sector and to non-profit work: industries where careful analysis can shape a better future. I'm also open to other opportunities where I can grow and contribute.
