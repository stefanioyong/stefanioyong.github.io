About me

From Jakarta to Seattle, chasing a dream one step at a time.

I was born and raised in Jakarta, Indonesia, and came to the United States on my own to pursue my dream and build my career. I've been in Seattle ever since, and I've fallen in love with it. Starting over in a new country taught me to adapt quickly, work hard and stay resilient.

I began my career in accounting, where I learned to read the story behind the numbers and to care about getting them right. Now I'm pursuing a Master of Science in Computational Finance and Risk Management at the University of Washington, bringing that foundation into analyst, risk management and quantitative roles.

I'm drawn to the energy sector and to non-profit work, where careful analysis can shape a better future. I want to know that at the end of the day, I've helped build something meaningful. I'm also open to other opportunities where I can grow and contribute.
